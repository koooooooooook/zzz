# Chapter 2: The Salt Hour (revised)

The lamp guttered twice before Kael understood that the sea was answering it, each flash of the great light returning as a pale pulse far out on the water. He pressed his forehead to the cold glass and counted the intervals, because counting was the only ritual that had ever kept the panic at bay. Somewhere below, in the gallery where the foghorn should have been, something began to breathe in time with the beacon.
